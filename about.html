About Jagruti Security Services

Your trusted partner in security and manpower solutions since 2010

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Jagruti Security Services Private Limited has grown from a small security agency to a comprehensive manpower solutions provider serving clients across multiple sectors.

We started with a simple mission: to provide reliable, professional security services that clients can trust. Over the years, we've expanded our offerings to include a wide range of manpower solutions while maintaining our commitment to quality and excellence.

Why Choose Us?

  • 14+ years of industry experience
  • 500+ trained professionals
  • 24/7 customer support
  • Rigorous background verification
  • Continuous training programs
  • Customized solutions for each client
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified operations
